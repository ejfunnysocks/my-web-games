📖 How to Play

  • 🏗️
    Cyber Tower — Sit back and watch it auto-aim and fire at enemies. Hold left click (or tap & drag) to manually take control and aim with a laser sight!
  • 🛸
    Invading Ships — spawn from outer edges. Watch out for rapid scouts and swarmers!
  • 📊
    Sector Levels — sector 1 has 5 sub-levels, sector 2 has 10, sector 3 has 15, and later sectors have 20. Gain XP from kills and survive to level up!
  • ⚡
    Energy Engine — each deployment costs 5 energy. Max energy is 30 (recharges 1 per 2 minutes, or bypass it in the shop).
  • 🃏
    Run Upgrades — choose a temporary card upgrade each time you Level Up to survive the sector. Upgrades pause gameplay but let the music roll!
  • 🔧
    Permanent Upgrades — run coins are banked at the end of a win or loss. Spend coins in Structure Upgrades and coins plus Research Points in the Research Lab.
  • 💎
    Demo Store — earn coins and gems, then spend them on cosmetics and upgrades. No real money is charged in this demo.
  • 💀
    Bosses — a five-second warning precedes a three-phase boss. Boss Cards have a one-in-three chance to drop; discover and equip them from the Boss Cards screen.
  • ⚡
    Overclock — clear sector 50 to restart with harder enemies, better rewards, and special upgrades. Your permanent unlocks stay with you.
  • 🎁
    Optional Rewards — enable demo banner ads during a run for 1.5× coins, or choose a simulated reward ad after the report. No real ads play in this demo.
